Will cover: producing a probabilistic cost-per-kilogram estimate, why the
recommender optimizes net cost rather than titer, and how recovery cost is
modelled.

The short version of the second point: recovery cost is determined upstream — by
titer, strain and broth composition — but incurred downstream. An optimizer
maximizing titer can therefore move the true objective backwards. See `D13` in
[Decisions](../decisions.md).
